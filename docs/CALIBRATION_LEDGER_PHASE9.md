# Phase 9 — evidence-driven calibration foundation

Personalized call selection now carries a separate, inspectable ledger:

- call-style contribution
- defensive-user contribution
- situation contribution
- exact total
- an explicit statement that the total is an authored preference weight, not a gameplay success probability

Automated tests require the components to sum exactly and verify that long-yardage and short-yardage objectives select the intended supported alternative. This creates a stable calibration seam for future practice-mode results without changing the formation matchup score or treating unverified play art as evidence.

The adjustment plan also separates normal coaching adjustments from an optional preset. A one-button preset can no longer consume the three coaching-adjustment slots or appear to be the whole plan. Quick/deep, inside/outside leverage, and field/boundary conflicts resolve to one instruction instead of displaying contradictory settings.
